I saw Spotlight yesterday. Great movie. It makes you wonder what good movies you have ignored because they did not happen to win an Oscar.

Facebook is full of life nowadays. The problem is we die, right? And that’s natural. What if we forgot to log out from Facebook before we did that? It’s not the first thing you think about I suppose. Whatever. Everyone does it. And there will come a day when dead people on Facebook will outnumber the living. [Someone calculated that date](http://fusion.net/story/276237/the-number-of-dead-people-on-facebook-will-soon-outnumber-the-living).

While alive though we are smartphone dependant even when we are together. I am not the first guy who glues his face on the screen while in a group. I am the third and I am not Facebooking. [I assert my dominance over Email like a BOSS](https://www.gq.com/story/email-flexing). If you don’t like it, what can you do about it? [Here is what](http://techcrunch.com/2016/03/04/how-to-politely-ask-people-to-get-the-fck-off-their-phones). It’s not good to be so religiously connected to these devices anyway.
