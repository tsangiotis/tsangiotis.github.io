## People are dying fast

It looks like [half a million people who should not be dying, are dying](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national/health-science/a-group-of-middle-aged-american-whites-is-dying-at-a-startling-rate/2015/11/02/47a63098-8172-11e5-8ba6-cec48b74b2a7_story.html). 40x the Ebola mortality rate and almost the mortality rate of HIV. These are middle-aged people with less than college education. The most likely suspects are “legal and illegal drugs, alcohol and suicide”.
