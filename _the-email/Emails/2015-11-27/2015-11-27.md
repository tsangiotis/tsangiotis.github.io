## Pseudoscience
Nike Air Max shoes are still a thing. People love them and Nike claims that they are the best thing that happened to your legs. It sounds fishy, doesn't it? Here is [the absurd history of Nike Air technology](http://gizmodo.com/the-brilliant-but-absurd-history-of-nike-air-technology-1741712594). 

Running shoes advice: You should feel rock solid with them and don't sweat your choice too much.

You should not believe everything you see on TV. [Forensic science is pretty unreliable](http://bostonreview.net/books-ideas/nathan-robinson-forensic-pseudoscience-criminal-justice). This has resulted in innocent people getting convicted and spending their lives in prison for crimes they did not commit. Long story sort, if you (wrongfully) find yourself in a court, hope that the real perpetrator was careful.
