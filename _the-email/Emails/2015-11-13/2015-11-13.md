## Startups go NASA
You have a US based advanced technology startup? If yes, [you can use NASA patents for free](http://theinstitute.ieee.org/ieee-roundup/opinions/ieee-roundup/nasa-offers-its-patent-licenses-to-startup-companies). These patents apply from biotechnology all the way to software development. However when you start to make money you will pay like everyone else. Don't be a freeloader!
