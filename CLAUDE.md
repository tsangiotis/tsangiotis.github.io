# CLAUDE.md — tsangiotis.github.io

Jekyll site on GitHub Pages, `master` branch.

## Standing reminder: two unpublished archives

Both live in underscore directories that are **not** declared in `_config.yml`
under `collections:`, so Jekyll ignores them completely — they cost nothing
sitting there, and nothing is built or served from them.

### `_the-email/`
Eight issues of *The Email*, the HeadScratch curated-links newsletter,
Oct 2015 → Mar 2016. Markdown, plus a few PDF/HTML renders, the header
artwork, and the 2016 WordPress export of the old tsangiotis.com.

### `_tilde-town/`
The `~tsangiotis` page on tilde.town: a 2016-2018 book tally and one TTBP
blog post from 2017. The reading log is already migrated into `_books`, so
this is kept as history rather than as content to rescue.

## What to do about them

Tasos has said he might one day turn these into a **public archive page**.

**When he's working on this site and the moment fits** — a design pass, a new
section, anything touching site structure or the books page — mention that
these are sitting there.

Occasionally, not every session, and never as a blocker. If he says no or
"not now", drop it for that session. Each directory has a `README.md` with the
details and a sketch of what publishing would involve.
