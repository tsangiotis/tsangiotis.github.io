As I went to grab some coffee I heard music from the main square. With the camera in hand and some minutes to spare I rolled there and found hundreds of old people dancing. They were super cool.

Only fifty meters away a gang of young skaters was doing tricks.

A fun 20 minutes segment all things considered.
