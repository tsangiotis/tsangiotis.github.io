# tilde.town — archive (unpublished)

Tasos's page on [tilde.town](http://tilde.town), the public-access unix
community. `~tsangiotis`.

## What's here

- `public_html/index.html` — **"Tasos Sangiotis' book tally"**, a reading
  ledger kept on tilde.town covering 2016, 2017 and 2018. 28 entries, with
  💚 marking the ones he liked.
- `public_html/blog/` — one post (2017-04-04) made with TTBP, the tilde.town
  blogging platform (`~endorphant/ttbp`), plus its purple `style.css`.

## Already migrated — this is a historical artifact, not lost content

The reading log **has already been absorbed into the site's `_books`
collection.** Counts line up year for year:

| Year | tilde.town | `_books` |
|---|---|---|
| 2016 | 17 | 17 |
| 2017 | 5 | 5 |
| 2018 | 6 | 5 |

The one extra 2018 row is *Alibaba: The House That Jack Ma Built* — the
tilde page lists it under **both** 2017 and 2018. `_books` has it once, which
is correct. `_books` also carries 2019-2022, which tilde.town never did.

So nothing here needs rescuing. It is kept for what it is: the predecessor of
the current books page, and a small piece of tilde.town history.

## Why it is not published

Underscore directory, not declared in `_config.yml` under `collections:`, so
Jekyll ignores it entirely.
