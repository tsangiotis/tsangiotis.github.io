I am in Rome with family!

Never made such series of posts (and in no way I guarantee that this would not be for today only) but as my timetable is not so strict and I actually have time to write it it is a good chance to keep my blogging habit.

Let's start...

First we visited <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piazza_Venezia" target="_blank" title="Link: http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piazza_Venezia">Piazza Venezia</a> and the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altare_della_Patria" target="_blank">Altare della Patria</a>.

Inside the Altare della Patria is located, the Monument of the Unknown Soldier.

Lot's of military history there.

We bought some Hop On - Hop Off tickets and we headed to <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piazza_Navona" target="_blank">Piazza Navona</a> and the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pantheon,_Rome" target="_blank">Pantheon</a>.

People with *Selfie Rods* running around taking pictures with the *Hole* while some were praying.

Here is a Photo Sphere:

\

<span class="small">[Views](https://www.google.com/maps/views/): [Pantheon Interior](https://www.google.com/maps/views/view/109316428502629371360/gphoto/6096390653518666802) by [Tasos Sangiotis](https://www.google.com/maps/views/profile/109316428502629371360 "Link: https://www.google.com/maps/views/profile/109316428502629371360")</span>

\

After that we had a great lunch at [Obicà Mozzarella Bar](http://obica.com/ "Link: http://obica.com/") and some awesome coffee at <a href="http://www.tazzadorocoffeeshop.com/" target="_blank" title="Link: http://www.tazzadorocoffeeshop.com/">Tazza D'Oro</a>.

\

Then some more rounds with the bus and we were back for a drink at the hotel.

\

That's it for today.
