Gear Acquisition Syndrome is a thing and it works if you clench your teeth and restrain yourself.\
\
When I got a new running watch on May 2024 I started running again. I got a Garmin Instinct 2X, black and white no-touchscreen G-SHOCK like behemoth which I fancy a lot.\
\
When I got it I run a lot. I also shed about 8kgs.\
\
When the heatwaves hit Athens this summer I stopped and all my progress fell apart and weight came back.\
\
Now end of August I started running again. And got a jump rope to avoid excuses.\
\
I learned to track my [VO2 Max](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VO2_max) for general health and wellbeing and saw many of the tables bellow.\
\
![](/assets/images/posts/i-just-started-running-again-after-i-just-started-running-again/01-VO2MaxChartforMen.jpg)\
I am in the no bad, no good territory.\
